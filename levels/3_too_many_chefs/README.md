# Too Many Chefs

The kitchens Part 4's 2v2 competition is played in. A set is played in one
drawn from here, by the contest server and by `cook match` alike.

Every kitchen is mirrored left to right, with two teams of two chefs: `reds`
(Ada and Bo) start on the left and `blues` (Cyd and Dev) on the right. Each
team has its own storages, stations and plate dispenser on its side, and
shares the delivery hatch in the middle and the order queue. Orders keep
coming for the whole game, and each team keeps its own score.

- `0_carrot_salad`: chop and plate; the quickest dish
- `1_coconut_juice`: a single chopped ingredient, with the hatch between the
  two sides
- `2_fruit_salad`: two chopped fruits combined, with open counters in the
  middle that either team can use
- `3_burgers`: grilling, slicing and stacking along one long counter that
  chefs can work from either side
