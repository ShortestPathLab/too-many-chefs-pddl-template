# Overcooked

Challenge levels with long recipes, multiple handoffs, and up to four chefs.

Black forest cake progresses through:

- `shared_stations_2p`: two chefs share every preparation stage
- `divided_3p`: ingredients and equipment are split across a pass counter
- `parallel_stations_4p`: four chefs have room to process several cakes at once
- `four_divided_4p`: batter, chocolate, baking, and decorating occupy separate
  quadrants
