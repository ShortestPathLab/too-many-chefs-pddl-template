# Too Many Chefs

The kitchen Part 4's 2v2 competition is played in, by the contest server and
by `cook match` alike.

`0_full_menu` is split along its length, with two teams of two chefs: `reds`
(Ada and Bo) start in the top half and `blues` (Cyd and Dev) in the bottom
half. Each team has its own storages, plate dispenser, bin and benches in its
half. The row of stations down the middle is shared and can be used from
either half: three cutboards, five cookers that each pair an oven with a
stove, a fryer, a mixer, and the delivery hatch. Both ends of that row are
open, as are the tiles either side of the hatch, so a chef can walk into
the other half. The teams also share the order queue. Orders keep coming for
the whole game, and each team keeps its own score.

Every dish from Parts 1 to 3 is on the menu: coconut juice, fruit salad and
parfait, hamburgers and cheeseburgers, sushi and fish-and-rice bowls, chicken
and chips, and black forest cake. A ticket's reward and time limit grow with
how long its dish takes, from 20 points and 50 steps for a coconut juice to
100 points and 180 steps for a cake.
